About the Initiative

The stroke awareness team

Battle Stroke is a community awareness effort focused on stroke prevention, symptom recognition, and faster action when emergencies happen. The team below is structured as an editable roster for your awareness campaign.

Mission

Why this work matters

Stroke can change a life in minutes. This project helps families, students, and local residents learn prevention habits and act immediately when warning signs appear.

Community Focus

What the team does

The awareness team supports education campaigns, school outreach, civic partnerships, and public reminders around the BE FAST stroke warning signs.
